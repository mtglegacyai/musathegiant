What Is Compounding and Why It Changes Everything

Compounding means your daily profits are automatically reinvested back into your trading capital. Instead of earning the same fixed amount every day, you earn a percentage of a growing balance. Your profits generate their own profits. Einstein reportedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world" and for good reason.

Without compounding: $1,000 earning 10% monthly = $100/month, every month. After 12 months you've earned $1,200 in profit.

With compounding: $1,000 earning 10% monthly, reinvested daily, grows exponentially. After 12 months your total balance could exceed $3,100. That's nearly 3x your original capital, from the same starting point.

Your Aurum dashboard has a built-in compounding calculator. Open it. Play with the numbers. See for yourself what compounding does to $100, $500, $1,000 over 6, 12, 24 months. The numbers will open your eyes.
