Common Questions About Aurum AI Bots

What is the minimum amount to start with any Aurum AI bot?

All three bots (EX-AI Bot, EX-AI PRO, and Zeus AI Bot) require a minimum deposit of $100 USDT. You also need a $19.99/year ecosystem access fee to unlock all Aurum products and the partner program.

What is the difference between EX-AI Bot and EX-AI PRO?

The EX-AI Bot is the flagship product with the highest average returns (17.5% monthly gross) and 8 profit-sharing tiers based on deposit size. EX-AI PRO is a flexible add-on available only to active EX-AI users, offering up to 10% monthly with no lock-up and full real-time trade visibility. Think of EX-AI as the long-term compounder and PRO as the flexible complement.

Can I run multiple bots at the same time?

Yes. You can run EX-AI Bot and Zeus AI Bot simultaneously as they operate independently. EX-AI PRO requires an active EX-AI deposit, so you can run all three at the same time if you meet the requirements. Each bot has its own deposit and withdrawal structure.

How do I withdraw my profits?

For EX-AI Bot, accumulated profits are available for withdrawal 24/7 and are processed within 48 hours. The minimum withdrawal is 25 USDT with a 1% fee. Principal withdrawals are available on the 1st, 10th, and 20th of each month (35% early withdrawal fee in the first year; no fee after one year). For Zeus AI Bot and EX-AI PRO, there is no lock-up. Withdrawals are processed within 72 hours with a 1% fee.

Which bot earns partner program commissions?

The EX-AI Bot generates the most partner income streams: Direct Sales, Team Sales, Legacy Volume, Profitshare, and Compounding. EX-AI PRO and Zeus AI Bot only generate Profitshare. The Aurum Partner Program has 15 Legacy Levels with up to 18.5% direct sales commissions and up to 30% Profitshare.

Are the returns guaranteed?

No. All financial activities involve risk, and past performance does not guarantee future results. The 17.5% average monthly figure for EX-AI Bot is a historical average across 36 months. Some months were higher (23.52% in March 2024), some lower (9.31% in July 2023). Never allocate more funds than you are willing to lose. Always do your own research.

How does compounding work with the EX-AI Bot?

When you reinvest your monthly profits instead of withdrawing them, your deposit balance grows, and the next month's return is calculated on the larger amount. The Aurum Platform includes a built-in calculator to project returns with or without compounding. For example, a 10,000 USDT VIP deposit compounded for 12 months could grow to approximately 52,781 USDT based on projected returns. Actual results will vary based on market conditions.
