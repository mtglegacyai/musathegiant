The 30-second version

Five things. That's the whole plan.

  1. 1Two seats under everyone

    That is what 2x2 means. Every cardholder has two seats right under them.

  2. 2Seats fill like reading a book

    Top row first, left to right. Then the next row. No gaps.

  3. 3Every row doubles

    2, then 4, then 8, then 16. Twelve rows hold 8,190 people.

  4. 4You earn 0.1% per level

    On every top-up made by cardholders in the rows you qualify for.

  5. 5Load $100 a month

    Onto your own card. It stays your money. That keeps you paid.

Step 1 of 7

Two seats under everyone

  • "2x2" just means two by two. Every person has two seats directly under them.
  • The row right under you is Level 1. The row under that is Level 2, and so on.
  • The first two cardholders you refer usually sit in your Level 1 seats.
  • Only cardholders take a seat. Registering alone does not.
Step 2 of 7

Seats fill in reading order

Top to bottom, left to right, like reading a page. The numbers show the order. Try it.

Tip: tap any filled seat to let that person refer someone.

  • You
  • You referred them
  • Your team referred them
  • Spillover from above
Press You refer someone to start.

No gaps, ever

The system always uses the next free seat. You cannot pick a seat, and nobody can skip the line.

Your 3rd, 4th, 10th referral

Once your two seats are full, extra people you refer go to the next free seat further down. Nobody you bring is wasted.

You still get your commission

You always earn the direct commission for people you refer, wherever the system places them.

Step 3 of 7

Spillover: help from above

  • Spillover means people under you that you did not refer.
  • They come from people above you (your upline) who refer more than two. Their extras flow down into the next free seats, and some land under you.
  • Their top-ups count for your rewards, as long as they sit in a level you qualify for.
  • No direct commission for you on spillover. That goes to whoever referred them.
This is why Bellver says it is not a binary plan. There is no "weaker leg". You earn on everyone in your paid levels, left and right.

The big question

Why a 2x2 matrix?

Why would Bellver pay people like you and me? Six honest reasons.

1

A marketing strategy

Instead of paying for billboards, TV and social media ads, Bellver rewards the people who use the card and share it. Word of mouth, from people you already trust.

2

Paid from the top-up fee

Every top-up carries a 3.9% fee. Part of it is shared with the matrix, so monthly rewards come from real people using a real card.

3

Rewards the builders

People who share the card and help others earn monthly income, again and again, for as long as they stay qualified and keep their seat.

4

Built to last

A company that earns from everyday card use can pay its staff, its partners and its rewards, month after month. That is sustainability.

5

Teamwork

Thanks to spillover, the people above you help fill your matrix, and you help the people below you. Nobody builds alone.

6

Simple to share

You only need two. Two is easy to explain, easy to copy and easy to teach.

Remember: the $100 you load every month is not a subscription. It is your own money, on your own card, for your own life. The 3.9% top-up fee is the only cost, and it is what keeps the card and the rewards going.

Step 7 of 7

Stay paid: the $100 rule

One condition, every month: load at least $100 onto your own card.

It is not a fee

The $100 is your own money on your own card, to spend however you like. A week of groceries covers it.

The only cost is the 3.9% top-up fee. It keeps Bellver running long term: team, offices and payment partners.

Miss one month

You lose that month's rewards in full. Next month, load $100 and you are paid again.

Miss three in a row

Your seat in the matrix is gone for good. Rewards built up until then move up to the next qualified person. You cannot get the old seat back.

Get your own card first. Your matrix seat starts when your card is bought. If someone you refer buys before you do, they are not placed in your matrix. You still earn their direct commission.

When the money arrives

  1. During the month

    Cardholders in your levels load their cards.

  2. Month end

    Bellver adds it all up. Top-ups in the last 7 business days roll into next month.

  3. The 15th

    Payday. Rewards arrive in USDT.

  4. Your choice

    Use it to fund your card, or withdraw once you have $100 or more.

Myths vs facts

The eight things people get wrong most often. Tap each one.

Myth"It is a binary plan. I only get paid on my weaker side."

FactNo. There is no weaker leg. You earn on every cardholder in your paid levels, left and right.

Myth"I have to recruit lots of people."

FactTwo direct referrals unlock your first star. More referrals mean more commissions, and they fill seats under you, but two is the starting point.

Myth"Signing up takes a seat."

FactOnly cardholders take seats. A sign-up without a card is not placed.

Myth"A Basic card earns monthly rewards."

FactNo. Basic earns direct commissions only. Monthly rewards start with Premium.

Myth"I can choose where people sit."

FactNo. The system places everyone in the next free seat, top to bottom, left to right.

Myth"The $100 a month is a fee."

FactNo. It is your own money, loaded onto your own card, yours to spend.

Myth"If I upgrade later, I get paid what I missed."

FactNo. Rewards are not back-dated. They count from your upgrade onwards.

Myth"The big numbers are guaranteed."

FactNo. Rewards depend on real top-ups. Bellver's examples assume every seat is full and everyone loads $300 a month.

Bellver's big examples, honestly
