What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser to remember things about you, like a login or your browsing activity.

Cookies this website sets

None. musathegiant.com has no logins, no analytics and no ads, so it has no reason to store cookies in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Google states that the Fonts service does not set cookies. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • Public blockchain explorers answer the halving page’s requests for block heights. These requests don’t carry cookies.

Websites I link to

When you click through to WhatsApp, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Bitcoin Wealth, ClickBaitPays or other sites, those websites may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How to control cookies

Every modern browser lets you view, block and delete cookies in its settings. Blocking cookies will not affect how this website works.
