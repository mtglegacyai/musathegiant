FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the Aurum Partner Program, income streams, and how to get started.

How much can I earn with the Aurum Partner Program?

Your earning potential depends on your activity level and the Legacy Level you achieve. Direct sales commissions go up to 18.5% on all personal product sales. Team sales generate ongoing difference bonuses paid endlessly through your structure. Profitshare reaches up to 30% daily from your team's AI bot profits. Extra Bonuses are paid upon reaching new Legacy Levels and can total up to $3,000,000. Shareholder Bonuses add quarterly income from the global EX-AI Bot pool starting at the Aurum Oracle level.

What is a Legacy Level in the Aurum Partner Program?

A Legacy Level is your rank within the Aurum partner structure. There are 15 Legacy Levels from Aurum Nova (entry level, 3% legacy percentage) up to Aurum Alpha (top level, 18.5% legacy percentage). Your Legacy Volume (LV) determines your level, and LV equals 40% of total team sales. Higher levels unlock higher commission percentages, profitshare rates, and one-time Extra Bonuses.

What is the Profitshare and when do I qualify?

Profitshare is a daily passive income stream where you earn a percentage of your direct partners' and clients' profits from the EX-AI Bot, EX-AI PRO Bot, Zeus AI Bot, and Aurum Card transaction fees. You begin earning Profitshare from the Aurum Voyager level (3,000 LV, 5% Profitshare) up to Aurum Alpha level (30% Profitshare). This income grows automatically as your team's trading volume increases.

What products qualify for Direct Sales commissions?

Direct Sales commissions (up to 18.5%) apply to EX-AI Bot deposits, compounds, and purchases, as well as Aurum Flash Loan purchases and Aurum Card issuance fees. Products such as EX-AI PRO Bot, Zeus AI Bot, and the API Bot contribute to Profitshare only, not Direct Sales commissions.

What is the Team Sales (Difference Bonus) and how does it work?

The Team Sales Difference Bonus allows you to earn the difference between your own Legacy Level percentage and the highest Legacy Level percentage within your team. This bonus is paid on all team sales, endlessly deep through your entire structure. For example, if your legacy percentage is 9% and your strongest team member has a 7% legacy percentage, you earn 2% on all sales made through that team member's entire downline.

Is there a cost to join the Aurum Partner Program?

Access to the Aurum Foundation ecosystem requires a $19.99 per year membership fee. There are no large upfront costs required to start building a partner business. The more you invest personally through purchasing Aurum products, the more Legacy Volume you accumulate, which advances your Legacy Level and unlocks higher income streams.

What is the Aurum Shareholder Bonus?

The Aurum Shareholder Bonus is a quarterly income stream available to partners who reach Aurum Oracle level and above. Every quarter, up to 3.175% of global EX-AI Bot deposit profits are allocated to qualifying Legacy Levels. Your share is distributed proportionally among all partners holding the same level. If you are the only qualifying partner at your level, you receive the full allocation. The second payout was distributed on January 16, 2026.

How do I get started with the Aurum Partner Program?

To get started, visit the Aurum Foundation backoffice using partner code EXTHT1. Create your account, complete the $19.99/year membership, and you can immediately begin building your team, distributing products, and earning commissions across all 6 income streams.
