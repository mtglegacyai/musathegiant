Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aurum Foundation? +

Aurum Foundation is an AI-driven fintech ecosystem that combines autonomous trading bots, the Aurum NeoBank, Aurum crypto debit cards, Aurum Exchange, Real-World Assets (including RWA Gold Packages with tokenized gold and discounted gold futures), and a global partner program into one unified platform. The idea began in February 2023, and Aurum Foundation Limited was officially registered in Hong Kong on November 7, 2024. It now serves over 200,000 partners worldwide.

What is the EX-AI Trading Bot? +

The EX-AI Bot is an autonomous AI-powered trading system that scans crypto markets 24/7, executing strategic spot trades to generate consistent returns with minimal risk. It uses advanced algorithms and machine learning to adapt to market conditions in real time.

Is Aurum Foundation legitimate? +

Aurum operates through four registered legal entities (Poland, UAE, Hong Kong, and Canada), manages over $600M in assets, serves 200,000+ partners globally, and has been featured in Forbes, Binance News, Benzinga, and other major publications. The company is led by experienced fintech professionals.

Who is behind Aurum? +

Aurum is led by CEO Bryan Benson, a Web3 veteran with 27+ years of experience and a former Binance executive. The leadership team of 37+ professionals spans financial markets, blockchain technology, AI, and network development.

When was Aurum established? +

Aurum Foundation started as an idea in February 2023 and was officially established through the registration of AURUM Foundation Limited in Hong Kong on November 7, 2024. It has since rapidly grown into a globally recognized fintech ecosystem with institutional-grade trading infrastructure and digital banking services.

What is the minimum to get started? +

Account creation is free. A $20 per year subscription unlocks full platform access, and $100 activates the AI bot. Trading capital starts from $100 to $100,000 per account. Users can top up with any amount thereafter, so it is flexible based on your personal investment capacity and risk tolerance.

Do I need to recruit people to earn? +

No. You can earn solely through the EX-AI Bot and other trading tools without recruiting anyone. The Partner Program is completely optional and offers additional commission streams for those who choose to participate.

Can I withdraw my earnings and capital anytime? +

Yes. Aurum provides flexibility to withdraw your profits at any time with a standard 1% withdrawal fee. Capital withdrawals made within the first 12 months carry a 35% fee. After 12 months, capital can be withdrawn at the normal 1% fee. Specific processing times apply depending on the product. Check the Aurum Back Office for current withdrawal schedules and thresholds.

What is the minimum withdrawal? +

The minimum withdrawal from the profits made from the EX-AI Trading Bot and the partner program is $25. Other withdrawal minimums depend on the specific product and withdrawal method. Consult the Aurum Back Office for current thresholds and processing details.

How do I get started? +

Click "Join Now" on this page and create your free account. Next, activate the $20 per year subscription to unlock full platform access. Then, deposit a minimum of $100 into your EX-AI Bot to start actual trading. Once funded, the bot begins trading for you within 12 to 24 hours. The entire setup takes just minutes.

What are Aurum RWA Gold Packages? +

Aurum RWA Gold Packages are real-world asset investment packages now available inside the Aurum ecosystem. Each package splits capital 50/50: half is deployed into AI-driven trading on tokenized gold (XAU) pairs, and half is allocated to discounted gold futures with a 12-month realization period and a 30% structural discount on gold. Packages start from 100 USDT and are activated directly through the Aurum Backoffice.

How do I activate an Aurum RWA Gold Package? +

Log into your Aurum Backoffice, navigate to the RWA Gold section, select your preferred package level (starting from 100 USDT), and follow the activation steps. Each package locks for 12 months and includes the 30% structural discount on gold futures. Multiple package levels are available, allowing you to scale your gold-backed position over time.
