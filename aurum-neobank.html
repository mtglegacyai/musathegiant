FAQ

Aurum NeoBank Questions Answered

What is Aurum NeoBank?

Aurum NeoBank is a next-generation financial platform that bridges traditional finance with digital assets. It operates through a Telegram-based interface and offers crypto-powered Mastercard debit cards, wallet management, AI bot access, and a partner referral system. Full-featured iOS and Android apps are planned to further expand accessibility.

What Aurum Card tiers are available?

Aurum offers four card tiers: Aurum Nova (33 USDT, 15,000 USDT/month limit), Aurum Imperium (66 USDT, 30,000 USDT/month limit), Aurum World Elite (99 USDT, 50,000 USDT/month limit, no KYC), and Aurum Infinity (249 USDT, 100,000 USDT/month limit, no KYC, physical card included). All cards support Apple Pay and Google Pay for contactless payments.

How do I earn commissions with Aurum NeoBank?

Aurum partners earn from four commission streams: Card Sales Commissions (up to 100% of the allocated margin from card sales within your network), Transaction Fees (up to 0.5% on every transaction), Currency Conversion Fees (up to 1% on each conversion), and USDT Top-Up Fees (up to 2.2% on deposits). Your commission percentage depends on your Legacy Level, starting at 35% (Nova) and scaling up to 100% (Alpha).

Do Aurum Cards require KYC verification?

It depends on the tier. Aurum Nova and Aurum Imperium require KYC verification. Aurum World Elite and Aurum Infinity do not require KYC verification, offering enhanced financial privacy. All tiers provide full Mastercard functionality regardless of KYC status.

Can I use Aurum Cards with Apple Pay and Google Pay?

Yes. All four Aurum Card tiers support both Apple Pay and Google Pay for contactless payments, both online and at physical point-of-sale terminals. The Aurum Infinity card also includes a physical Mastercard for traditional in-store transactions and ATM access.

What is the Legacy Volume system?

Legacy Volume determines your partner rank and commission percentage. Card sales contribute to Legacy Volume using the 40% rule: 100,000 USDT in card sales within your structure counts as 40,000 USDT in personal Legacy Volume. Each individual card generates a Legacy Volume of 150 USDT. There are 15 Legacy Levels from Nova (35% bonus) to Alpha (100% bonus), each unlocking higher commission rates, extra bonuses, and increased earning potential.

Is there a cost to become an Aurum NeoBank partner?

There are no initial costs or risks to start earning as an Aurum NeoBank partner. You can begin distributing cards and earning commissions immediately. Access to the full Aurum Foundation ecosystem (including AI trading bots and the partner program) requires a $19.99/year membership fee through the back office.
