My story

My background is in IT support. Years of fixing systems taught me to look under the hood before trusting anything, and that habit followed me into crypto.

I started exploring Bitcoin and digital income because I wanted more than a single salary for my family. Along the way I built a community, learned a lot, and made mistakes. The biggest lesson: not every opportunity is what it claims to be. That is why I now explain how things work before I share them, and I only share what I’m using myself.

By nature I’m a quiet, down-to-earth introvert. Leading a community did not come naturally. I learned it on purpose, because I believe people deserve someone who will explain things clearly and tell them the truth.

What I check before I share anything

  • I use it myself. I don’t recommend anything I haven’t joined and tested with my own money.
  • I understand where the money comes from. If I can’t explain it simply, I don’t share it.
  • Withdrawals work. I check that money can actually come out, not just go in.
  • The terms are clear. Costs, rules and risks need to be written down where you can read them.
  • You decide. I explain, you choose. Nobody should feel pressured into anything.

Even with these checks, every opportunity carries risk and things can change. Please read my risk disclaimer and only use money you can afford to lose.
