Who we are

musathegiant.com is the personal website of Musa The Giant (“MTG”, “I”, “me”), based in Durban, South Africa. I am responsible for the information described in this policy, in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What this website collects

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-up forms, no accounts, no comments and no newsletter. It does not run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or any other tracking or advertising tools.

Services that see your visit

Like every website, some technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) reaches the services that deliver the pages:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google’s servers. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • Public blockchain explorers. On the halving countdown, your browser requests current block heights from public services such as mempool.space. They receive a normal web request from your browser.

When you contact me

If you message me on WhatsApp or social media, I receive your name, number or username and whatever you choose to send. I use it only to reply to you. I don’t sell it, rent it or add you to bulk lists. Those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Links to other websites

This site links to platforms such as Bitcoin Wealth, ClickBaitPays, YouTube, TikTok and Facebook. Once you leave musathegiant.com, their privacy policies apply, not this one.

Children

This website is meant for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and I don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask what personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, and object to its use. If you are unhappy with my response, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update it on this page and change the “last updated” date above.
