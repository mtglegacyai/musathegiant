What each coloured field means
Yellow: wallet balanceYour USDT and USDC balance in your dynamic wallet. Other coins are not shown here. You pay for cards from this balance.
Green: this month's earningsDirect commissions and rewards earned in the current billing month.
Red: missed rewardsRewards you did not receive because you were not qualified in time. Ideally always zero.
Blue: card balanceThe money currently on your card, or on all your cards combined.
Grey: lifetime totalsEverything you have ever earned in commissions and rewards.
Dark blue: earnings availableOnce a month the green fields move here, ready to withdraw. Payout day is the 15th.
Menu by menu

My Wallet: your Fireblocks wallet. Swap currencies, send funds, set a transaction password and export your private key under Settings, then Account and security.

Deposit: fund with crypto (USDT or USDC recommended, plus BNB or TRX for gas), credit card, or SEPA and PayPal in Europe. A calculator shows how much you need.

Withdraw: request payouts of $100 or more. They show as pending, then appear in My Wallet.

My Cards: order cards and see each card's balance and transactions. When your card appears you get the next free place in the 2x2 matrix.

Referral: your matrix, direct referrals, people per level, card levels and ranks in your team, including spillover.

Settings: switch on two-factor authentication. Bellver strongly recommends it.

The top-up maths

The 3.9% top-up fee is added on top of the amount you load, so the wallet must hold the amount plus the fee. That is why Max fails.

  • Max: $15.60 + $0.61 fee = $16.21, more than the $15.60 in the wallet.
  • The fix: take the fee off first. $15.60 minus $0.61 = $14.99.
  • Load $14.99: fee $0.59 (Bellver rounds it up), total $15.58. It fits.

Quick rule: the most you can load is roughly your wallet balance divided by 1.039. Figures from the recording on 17 October 2026; fees can change, so always read the breakdown before you tap Top up.
