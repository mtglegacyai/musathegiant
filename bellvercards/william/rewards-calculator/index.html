How it is worked out

Every time a cardholder in your paid levels tops up their card, you receive 0.1% of that top-up. So $1,000 loaded pays you $1. The calculator multiplies cardholders by their average top-up, then takes 0.1%.

Where the cardholders come from

Your rank decides how many levels you are paid on. Seats fill top to bottom, left to right, and spillover from above can land under you. See the 2x2 matrix
